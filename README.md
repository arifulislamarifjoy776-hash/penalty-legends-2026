### 🌟 Welcome to Penalty Legends!

*Penalty Legends 2026* is an interactive football penalty shootout game developed using **HTML, CSS, and JavaScript**.

The game combines team selection, player customization, animated football graphics, goalkeeper AI, and penalty shootout challenges into a fun browser-based experience.

No installation or external libraries are required. Just open the game and start playing!

## 🎮 How to Play

**Step 01 — Choose Your Team**

Select your favorite national football team and choose an opponent.

**Step 02 — Customize Your Player**

Enter your player's name, select a jersey number, and choose your kit.

**Step 03 — Enter the Stadium**

Click **ENTER THE STADIUM** to start the penalty shootout.

**Step 04 — Aim Your Shot**

Choose one of the nine shooting zones or click directly on the goal.

**Step 05 — Set Your Power**

Adjust the power slider to control your kick strength. Be careful: too much power can reduce accuracy.

**Step 06 — Take the Penalty**

Click **TAKE THE SHOT** and watch the goalkeeper react.

**Step 07 — Win the Championship**

Score more penalties than your opponent to become the champion!

## 🚀 Run the Game Locally

1. Download or clone this repository.
2. Open the project folder in VS Code.
3. Open `index.html` in your browser.
4. Select your team and start playing!

The game runs locally without an internet connection.

---

## 🧠 How the Game Works

The penalty system uses JavaScript to determine shot outcomes based on selected direction, power, goalkeeper positioning, and difficulty.

The Canvas API handles the football graphics and animations, while CSS controls the overall game interface.

The match follows a five-penalty format, with sudden death if both teams remain tied.
